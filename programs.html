Six pathways to brighter futures

Organize BFF's work into clear program areas that visitors can understand and support.

01

Education and Second-Chance Opportunities

BFF will support girls and young people whose education has been interrupted by poverty, early marriage, family circumstances or other challenges. Activities may include:

  • Education re-entry support
  • Academic and life-skills support
  • Mentorship
  • Career guidance
  • Functional literacy and numeracy
  • School-community engagement
  • Scholarship and educational opportunity referrals
  • Connecting young people with education partners

The programme will particularly seek to ensure that dropping out of school does not have to mean giving up on one's future.

Program details
02

Vocational Skills and Entrepreneurship

BFF will support young women and youth to acquire practical skills that can lead to employment, self-employment and enterprise development. Potential areas include:

  • Tailoring and fashion
  • Baking and food processing
  • ICT and digital skills
  • Beauty and cosmetology
  • Handcrafts
  • Agro-processing
  • Agriculture-related enterprises
  • Other locally relevant skills

Training will be linked to entrepreneurship, financial literacy, business planning, market access and mentorship.

Program details
03

Economic Empowerment and Revolving Support

BFF will seek to connect trained participants with opportunities to turn their skills into sustainable livelihoods. Depending on available resources and partnerships, support may include:

  • Entrepreneurship training
  • Business mentorship
  • Business plan development
  • Market linkages
  • Seed capital
  • Equipment and productive assets
  • Savings and financial literacy
  • A revolving fund for eligible graduates

The revolving fund will be designed so that repayments from supported enterprises can help finance subsequent beneficiaries, creating a cycle of opportunity rather than a one-time intervention.

Program details
04

Agroecology and Green Livelihoods

BFF will promote agroecology as a pathway towards food security, environmental sustainability, climate resilience and improved rural livelihoods. Activities may include:

  • Farmer training on agroecological practices
  • Organic farming
  • Soil health management
  • Crop diversification
  • Production and use of organic farm inputs
  • Farmer-led experimentation
  • Youth and women-led green enterprises
  • Biodiversity conservation
  • Environmental awareness
  • Market opportunities for sustainable agricultural products

BFF will also explore Farmer-Led Living Labs, where farmers, extension workers, researchers, advisors and other stakeholders work together to identify challenges, test solutions and share knowledge. Farmers will not only be recipients of knowledge; they will become co-creators, innovators and knowledge holders.

Program details
05

Mentorship, Volunteerism and Giving Back

The BFF Giving-Back Movement will provide a platform for individuals to contribute to community development using whatever resources they possess. Volunteers may contribute through:

  • Mentorship
  • Career guidance
  • Counselling
  • Training
  • Community journalism and storytelling
  • ICT and digital support
  • Entrepreneurship coaching
  • Agricultural knowledge
  • Leadership development
  • Research
  • Fundraising
  • Networking
  • Community mobilisation

BFF will encourage programme beneficiaries who successfully progress to eventually give back to others. For example, a girl supported to return to school may later mentor another girl. A young woman supported to establish a business may later train another entrepreneur. A farmer who learns successful agroecological practices may teach other farmers A professional who once benefited from mentorship may become a mentor. This creates a continuous cycle: Receive → Grow → Succeed → Give Back → Empower Another.

Program details
06

Community Storytelling and Journalism

BFF will also create opportunities for volunteer journalists, writers, photographers, videographers and storytellers to use their skills for community development. The initiative will document stories of:

  • Educational transformation
  • Young women rebuilding their lives
  • Youth entrepreneurship
  • Farmer innovation
  • Agroecological practices
  • Community leadership
  • Volunteerism
  • People who were once supported and are now supporting others.

BFF believes that telling someone's story can itself be an act of giving back.
