BRIGHT FUTURES FOUNDATION — 7-PAGE WEBSITE
================================================

Pages:
1. index.html          Home
2. about.html          About Us
3. programs.html       Programs (six program areas)
4. team.html           Our Team
5. beneficiaries.html  Beneficiaries / beneficiary stories
6. news-videos.html    News & Videos
7. donate.html         Donation + volunteer form

FILES:
- style.css = all design styling
- script.js = mobile menu and small interactive features
- images/ = local placeholders

EDITING IN NOTEPAD++:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open any .html file in Notepad++.
3. Change text between HTML tags.
4. Replace images in the images folder while keeping the filenames, or edit the image paths.
5. Open index.html in a browser to test locally.
6. Upload all files/folders to your hosting public_html/www folder.

IMPORTANT BEFORE LAUNCH:
- Replace placeholder logo and photos.
- Replace sample statistics and team names.
- Add your official physical address and approved organizational information.
- Connect the donation button to your actual approved payment gateway.
- Connect volunteer/newsletter forms to a secure form-processing/email service.
- Replace social media # links with your official profile URLs.
- Add real YouTube/Vimeo embed codes in the video section.
- Obtain appropriate consent before publishing beneficiary photographs or personal stories.

BRANDING:
The design uses a professional green, yellow and brown palette inspired by the Bright Futures Foundation branding discussed previously, with bold modern sans-serif typography.
